PreAuth

The PreAuth project for Shiloh Finance took a loan application and predicted whether the underwriters would approve it or not and at what tier.

Description

The classifier was trained on ~400 labeled prior applications, compiled in JSON format. It evaluated multiple decision tree models, including XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting), LightGBM (Light Gradient Boosting Machine), and CatBoost (Categorical Boosting). It had ~30 features, most of which required extraction from PDF files.

Efficacy

Shiloh Finance (also known as ShiFi) is a US‑based financial technology company that provides in‑house financing solutions, primarily for high‑ticket sales like coaching and consulting services. They specialize in providing tailored payment plans for customers with a range of credit scores (from super-prime to sub-prime), allowing businesses to offer financing options to a broad client base. Their services include bookkeeping, reporting, payroll, and cash flow planning.
